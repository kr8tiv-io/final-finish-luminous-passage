## What this measures

Every media chapter, at every breakpoint, sampled in **0.5 s film steps**. At each step the script hides the reading zone's text with `visibility: hidden`, screenshots the viewport, restores the text, and takes the **per-channel median** of the pixels behind each text box. Contrast is WCAG 1.4.3 relative luminance, text colour against that median. Where an element paints its own background, that background is composited back in before judging — hiding it for the sample must not flatter it.

Thresholds: **4.5:1** body, **3:1** WCAG large text (>= 24 px, or >= 18.66 px at weight >= 700). A box whose background median is within 6/255 of the paper `#FAF9F6` on every channel is on the paper wash and is reported as `wash`, not flagged.

`median` is the headline number. `worst` is the lowest contrast among the 5th/25th/50th/75th/95th luminance percentiles of the same pixels — it catches a box that straddles a bright edge, where the median flatters it.

**This script does not fix anything.** Step 16 owns the reading-zone CSS.
