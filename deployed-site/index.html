A shop floor, a showroom, a shared corridor.

Explore commercial floors ↗

  1. The sequence

    From bare slab to finished floor.

    Six steps, in this order. Installation usually runs two days, and it varies with the concrete underneath.

    How a floor actually goes ↗
  2. 01

    A free estimate

    Send photos of the floor, or book a visit. Either works, and both are free. Denis wants to see the concrete, what is on it now, and any cracks you already know about.

  3. 02

    Grinding

    The slab is ground to open the surface so the coating can bond. It is loud, dusty work, and the room has to be empty before it starts.

  4. 03

    Cosmetic crack filling

    Visible cracks are filled so they do not read through the finish. If something turns up that is structural rather than cosmetic, you hear about it then, not later.

  5. 04

    The flake broadcast

    Flake is broadcast into the coat while it is still open. This is where the colour and the pattern of the floor are set.

  6. 05

    The scrape

    Once it has set, the surface is scraped back. That is what turns a loose broadcast into a flat floor you can walk on and clean.

  7. 06

    The top coat

    The last coat goes over everything. Where a room needs more grip, an anti-slip top coat goes on instead, and that is a decision we make with you.

Planning an Edmonton floor coating

Before you choose a colour.

A useful floor starts with a useful conversation. Tell us what the space needs to do, what the concrete looks like now, and what you would like to change.

01What condition is your concrete in?

Dust, cracks, pitting and an existing coating are all worth mentioning before an Edmonton garage-floor assessment. Include photographs of the whole room and close-ups of the areas that concern you. The preparation discussion comes before the final finish recommendation.

Understand concrete preparation ↗
02How will you use the room?

Parking through winter, working at a bench and setting up a basement fitness area call for different conversations. Talk through wet footwear, cleaning, traction, equipment and the look you want. A basement coating is not a substitute for addressing moisture or planning the rest of a renovation.

Explore garage and home floor coatings ↗
03What helps Denis prepare your quote?

Share your location, approximate floor area, current surface and preferred way to be contacted. Tell us about access, items that need moving and any schedule constraints. Preparation, repair and finish choices shape the scope; an attractive sample on screen is the start of the decision, not a final specification.

Tell Denis about your space ↗
04Planning a shop or commercial floor?

Start with the working day: traffic, cleaning routines, equipment, access and when the area can be taken out of use. Bring those details into the assessment so the conversation covers both the finished appearance and the practical installation plan.
