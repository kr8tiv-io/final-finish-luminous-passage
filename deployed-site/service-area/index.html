Edmonton first

Edmonton is home, and most of the work is here.

The company was founded by Denis, and it does quality floor makeovers in Edmonton, Alberta. There is no showroom to visit. We come to the concrete, look at it, and quote from what is actually there.

The photographs in the project archive are Edmonton floors: garages, workshops, and shop floors, shot before preparation and after coating.

Outside the city

Ask whether we travel to your area.

We do go out of town for some jobs. Whether we come to you depends on the drive, the size of the floor, and what else is booked that week.

This page does not carry a list of towns. A list would suggest we already work in every one of them, and we would rather tell you the real answer on the phone. Call Denis at (780) 991-1651, give him the address, and he will say yes or no.

What helps

Tell us where it is and what it has to hold.

A free estimate can start from photos or from a visit. Either way, the useful details are the same.

  • Where the space is, and whether there is a clear route in for equipment.
  • Roughly how big the floor is.
  • What is on it now: bare concrete, an old coating, or paint.
  • Anything you already know about cracks, oil, moisture, or damage.

From there we can talk about preparation, the finish, and what the job looks like.
